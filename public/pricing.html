Frequently asked questions

What is VendorLair?

VendorLair is web-based vendor management software for small businesses. It keeps supplier contacts, contract dates, categories, notes and follow-up actions in one place.

Is VendorLair free?

The Free plan supports up to 10 vendors. Pro costs $3 per month and supports up to 100 vendors.

What does the Action Centre show?

The Action Centre highlights vendor records that require action or attention, including upcoming contract deadlines and missing information.

Can I export my vendor list?

Yes. VendorLair includes CSV export so you can take your vendor records into a spreadsheet.

Do I need to install anything?

No. VendorLair runs in your web browser. Create an account and confirm your email to get started.

Is Pro unlimited?

No. The current Pro plan supports up to 100 vendors. If you need more, contact VendorLair before subscribing.

How do I start?

Create a free account, confirm your email, and add your first vendor. Start with the supplier name, a contact and any relevant contract expiry date.
