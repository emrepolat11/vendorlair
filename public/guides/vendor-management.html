How to manage vendors in a small business

Vendor management means keeping track of who supplies your business, what they provide, what you pay and what needs a decision. A useful starting point is one reliable vendor register and a regular review of upcoming deadlines.

1. Build a single vendor register

Start with active suppliers. Include software subscriptions, logistics providers, contractors and other services your business relies on. Give each record an owner so questions and renewal decisions have somewhere to go.

2. Review deadlines before they become urgent

Expiry and notice deadlines are different. Read the agreement to find the cancellation or renewal terms, then set a review date early enough to compare options. Use the renewal tracking checklist for a worked example.

3. Keep actions specific

Write actions as a task with an owner and a date: “Ask the account manager for next year’s pricing by 15 May” is easier to follow than “Review supplier”. Record the outcome after each conversation.

A manageable review routine

  • Weekly: check approaching deadlines and open actions.
  • Monthly: update contact details and close completed actions.
  • Before renewal: confirm current needs, pricing, notice terms and the decision owner.
